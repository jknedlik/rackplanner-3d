    Controls

    Orbit: drag to rotate · wheel to zoom · right-drag or Shift-drag to pan · click to inspect

    Walk: W A S D to move · Shift to run · E / Q up and down · mouse to look · click to inspect

    Rack row view: double-click a rack or device (or “Enter row mode” in the panel) for a straight-on view of the whole row, with every device labeled · wheel scrolls from rack to rack · Shift+wheel (or pinch) zooms · click a rack to switch to it · drag (or the Orbit button) orbits around the row while row mode stays on · the big “Leave row mode” button or Esc exits · the Row / Rack sliders at the left jump between the floor's rows (keeping your rack column, with a camera flight whose length the Flight slider sets — 3 s by default) and between the racks of the open row · the Zoom slider sets the magnification (px per U) · the “Fade” toolbar button dims device labels by distance (on by default)

    Common: V toggles walk / orbit · Esc deselects (or leaves walking) · floor buttons jump between floors
